TV Library Viewer

One Mac keeps your TV shows. Everything else views them from that Mac.

TV Library Viewer browses and plays the TV shows in your Mac's Apple TV app library — on that Mac, and shared over your own network to your other Macs, your iPad and iPhone, your Apple TV and your car. The shows never leave your devices: there's no cloud service and nothing to sign up for.

What it does

  • Reads the TV app's library and artwork directly, so it opens quickly and stays up to date.
  • Marks episodes watched, keeps resume points, and adds or removes episodes — all through the TV app, which stays the one record of your library. Video files are never deleted.
  • Shares the library with your other devices over your own network, each person keeping their own watching, full descriptions and cast.
  • Downloads episodes for offline viewing on a Mac, iPad or iPhone.

Download for Mac
