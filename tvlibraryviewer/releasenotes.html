Release Notes

What's new in each version, from the latest all the way back to the first. The Mac app updates itself; your other devices update through TestFlight.

Version 1.7 November 23, 2026 · build 164

  • 3D on every screen: on a Mac, iPad, iPhone and Apple TV, and in Safari, episodes can play in 3D, made by the server from the episode as it streams, as for Vision Pro. The new 3D button in the player turns it on or off where you are, with no question first, and the next episodes play the same way. On the Mac it's at the right of the title bar; on the iPad and iPhone at the top right, with the controls; on the Apple TV in the bar under the picture; in the web client by the player's other buttons.
  • 3D Settings, beside the 3D button: Viewing is how you see it: with Red/Cyan Glasses (the usual kind, red over the left eye), Red/Blue, Green/Magenta or Amber/Blue Glasses, or without glasses, side by side: Cross-Eyed (cross your eyes until the two pictures meet) or Parallel (look through the screen into the distance). Depth, Screen, Subtitles and Quality work as on Vision Pro, and a change carries on from where you are after a few seconds. Side by side, the subtitles showing when you turned 3D on are drawn into both pictures. A Mac playing its own library makes its 3D itself, whether or not it shares the library.
  • Mac: the player's controls fade away again a few seconds after you stop moving the pointer, in a small window too. In a small window (as side by side is watched) they covered nearly all of it, and stayed while the pointer was anywhere over them.

Version 1.6.1 November 22, 2026 · build 162

  • Apple TV: the Top Shelf lines up what to watch next: first the shows you've watched in the last 30 days, the one you played last first, then the other shows with episodes left, newest added first. Shows you've finished are left out, and drop off as soon as you finish them.
  • Vision Pro: in the theater, the next episode (played on when one ends) now goes onto the screen too. Moving Screen Height during it no longer leaves the sound playing with no picture. Changing the 3D settings twice in a row no longer can either.
  • Send Log to Server, on every device: with Keep a Log on, the log of what the player and the connection did goes to the server with one click, instead of being copied and pasted. The server keeps it in its Sent Logs folder (Help › Show Logs Sent to This Server on the Mac running it), so whoever looks after it can read it. The Apple TV now has Keep a Log too, in About. The log also notes how fast video was arriving when the player waits, so a slow network shows up plainly (an Apple TV that measured 0.9 Mbit/s until it was restarted, say).
  • Streaming no longer stops on a spinner partway through an episode (seen in 3D on Vision Pro) because another device at home kept failing to sign in. Every device at home reaches the Mac from the same internet address, and ten failed sign-ins from it refused all of them for a while, even one already watching. A device that signed in correctly now stays signed in while it uses the library; new wrong guesses are still refused. Each failed sign-in now appears in the account log with the device's name, so the one that keeps failing can be found.
  • An episode whose file has been moved, renamed or deleted no longer opens a player that waits on and on. On the Mac with the library, playing it says the file can't be found and offers Locate…, as Apple's TV app does: choose the file and the library uses it from then on, on every device. If other episodes of the show are missing their files too and the folder you chose has files with their names, it offers to use those as well. The iPad, iPhone, Apple TV, Vision Pro, the web client and other Macs say the Mac can't find the file, and where it was.
  • Vision Pro 3D: an episode no longer stops to spin partway through. When the Mac had made the 3D five minutes ahead and was waiting for the player to catch up, it took the quiet for no one watching and stopped, so the player ran dry and had to start the 3D over.
  • Vision Pro: in the theater, the controls fold away to a faint button low on your right while you watch, instead of a bright panel that was always there. Look at it and it brightens; select it to open the controls (smaller and closer to you than before), and their X to fold them away again.
  • Mac updates: each build is published as a file of its own, so an update can't be refused as "improperly signed" when a version is released again.
  • Vision Pro 3D: dragging the position slider back and forth, past where you wanted and back, no longer leaves the episode stuck. The Mac starts making 3D only once the slider has rested for half a second, instead of for every place you pass, and the player waits for it where you stopped instead of going back to where you started.
  • Vision Pro 3D: subtitles now sit on the screen instead of far in front of it, and a new Subtitles slider in the 3D settings moves them anywhere from in front of the screen to behind it. (Needs the Mac on this build and the Vision Pro app's next TestFlight build.)
  • Vision Pro: the player's Viewing tab opens at its full size with the 3D settings the first time, instead of as a small panel that came right only when opened again. In the theater its controls sit lower, below the screen.
  • Vision Pro: after 3D stopped and an episode went on in 2D, the next episodes no longer play in 2D without asking until the app is quit: the Mac answers whether it can make 3D at once, and the Vision Pro asks again if it doesn't hear back.
  • Security: the library's web address (through a proxy such as Caddy) counts wrong passwords for each visitor, so one visitor can't lock everyone out, and the library password can't be guessed endlessly. Changes can't be sent from another site's pages, and requests a proxy could misread are refused.
  • A show's picture uses the cover in its video files when the TV app has none of its own, instead of a frame from the video, and is looked for again when an episode or new artwork is added (an added episode's cover didn't show in the grid, even after restarting).
  • Web client: the language list scrolls all the way to its last language, on an iPhone too (the last ones were left under Safari's toolbar), and long menus fit the window.
  • Web client: with artwork set to Automatic, the shows appear already in their shapes, instead of standing tall and then shuffling down as each picture arrives.
  • The Mac app now needs macOS 26 or later, and shows Liquid Glass throughout: the Play, Resume and other buttons, and the toolbar. (In build 150 the buttons kept their old look.)
  • Mac and iPad: a show's page has its artwork, softly blurred, behind the title.
  • Apple Vision Pro: if the Mac can't make an episode in 3D just then, the player says so after about a minute and offers Watch in 2D, instead of waiting on and on.
  • Apple Vision Pro: the Viewing panel shows the theater and the 3D settings side by side, each in a section of its own, sized to fit its words in every language, so nothing is cut short and nothing needs scrolling. The ends of each slider are icons with their names under them, and Reset to Standard Settings is a button of its own.
  • Apple Vision Pro: taking the headset off while watching now pauses the episode; put it back on and it's there to go on with. (A 3D episode could be left black, without its controls.)
  • Liquid Glass buttons on the Mac, iPad and Apple TV, and on Apple Vision Pro the Viewing button above the player.

Version 1.6 November 18, 2026 · build 149

  • Apple Vision Pro: TV Library Viewer now runs on Apple Vision Pro. Watch In a Window, In the Theater (a movie theater around you: choose your seat and raise or lower the screen) or Outdoors, in the system's Environments.
  • Any episode in 3D on Apple Vision Pro: the Mac with your library turns the episode into 3D as it streams, with nothing to prepare ahead. Choose Watch in 3D or Watch in 2D when an episode starts. The player's Viewing panel (with the theater's seat and screen) sets the depth, whether the picture comes out in front of the screen or goes in behind it, and the quality; a change carries on from where you are.   • iPad: beside the sidebar in a narrow window, the bar keeps the page's title; controls that don't fit move into View Options.
  • CarPlay: fixed the app closing when a show was chosen from Recently Added.
  • Fixed a device sometimes offering to continue an episode already watched on another one.
  • Fixed QR codes showing blank when linking a device.

Version 1.5 November 11, 2026 · build 136

  • In the web client, a size button in the player. With the Alternative Video Player on, Automatic moves between Original, HD and SD as the connection changes.
  • On the Mac, iPad, iPhone and Apple TV, choose Video Quality (Original, Automatic, HD or SD) beside Language.
  • The size being played shows on the line under the title, with the player's controls.
  • Subtitles in the web client no longer need anything extra installed on the Mac.
  • The Alternative Video Player reads further ahead, so it rides out a slow patch.
