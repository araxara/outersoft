Release Notes

What's new in each version, from the latest all the way back to the first. The Mac app updates itself; your other devices update through TestFlight.

Version 1.3.2 November 9, 2026 · build 129

  • A new Accounts screen on the iPad, iPhone and Apple TV (About › Accounts) lists the accounts signed in on the device — switch between them without signing in again, sign one out, or add another.
  • On the iPad, the player's on-screen controls now come back reliably with a tap while you're watching full screen.
Earlier versionsHide earlier versions

Version 1.3.1 November 9, 2026 · build 127

  • Genres now appear under Recently Added, Recently Watched and Downloads too, not just the main show lists — pick a genre to narrow any of those views.
  • On the Apple TV, each setting opens as a full list you scroll with the remote, and the app goes straight to your shows right after you sign in.
  • Your signed-in account's name now shows in About on every device.
  • New setups show artwork in each show's own shape by default (the Automatic setting).

Version 1.3 November 8, 2026 · build 125

  • Sign in by scanning a QR code or entering a short code, on any device — so there's no password to type with the Apple TV remote.
  • Your account is now your email address, and you can reset a forgotten password by email.
  • A Paired Devices list shows everywhere you're signed in and lets you sign a device out.
  • The web client now shows episode titles and descriptions in your language, and reads better on a phone.
  • Your cast and translation keys moved to their own Keys tab in Settings.
  • Reliability fixes for watching in the car (CarPlay).

Version 1.2 November 2, 2026 · build 116

  • The Mac app now keeps itself up to date automatically.
  • New: open your library in a web browser, with nothing to install — including subtitles, resume points, changing your password, and seeing who's watching.
  • Genre names now appear in your language.
  • Fixes for watching in the car (CarPlay).

Version 1.1.2 November 1, 2026 · build 94

  • Smoother playback when several people watch from the same Mac at once.

Version 1.1.1 November 1, 2026 · build 92

  • Settings windows can be resized and remember their size.

Version 1.1 November 1, 2026 · build 90

  • Cast and crew, with photos and biographies, on every show page.
  • A built-in help book, in all 32 languages, in every app.
  • Episode titles and descriptions now appear in your language.
  • A new Settings › Storage for your library and keys.

Version 1.0 October 31, 2026 · build 83

  • Translated into 32 languages, with a language choice in every app.
  • Tooltips on the main controls explain what they do.
  • Episodes only the Apple TV app can play are now marked, rather than hidden.

Version 0.9 October 6, 2026 · build 80

  • Share your Mac's library across your home network to your other Macs, iPad, iPhone and Apple TV — your shows never leave your devices.
  • A new Apple TV app, with Top Shelf and show pages like the TV app.
  • A new iPad and iPhone app.
  • Each person gets their own account, with their own watching, resume points and downloads.
  • Download episodes to watch offline on a Mac, iPad or iPhone; Smart Downloads keep the next few ready.
  • Search your library, and find shows and episodes in Spotlight.
  • At the end of an episode, the next unwatched one plays on its own.
  • Player improvements: 10-second skip buttons, double-click to zoom, the screen stays awake, and playback recovers from stalls.
  • Tuned for slow and mobile connections, so shows keep playing away from home.
  • Watch in the car through CarPlay.
  • Sharing is encrypted, and you can reach a library on another network over SSH.

Version 0.1 October 2, 2026 · build 29 First version

  • Browse and play the TV shows in your Mac's Apple TV library.
  • Mark episodes watched and resume where you left off, with progress bars.
  • Add or remove episodes, seasons and shows — always through the TV app, so it stays the record of your library. Video files are never deleted.
  • Unwatched, Partially Watched, Completed and Recently Added views.
  • Full episode descriptions and a genre column, like the TV app.
  • Choose a System, Light or Dark appearance.
