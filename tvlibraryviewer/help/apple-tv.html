Apple TV

tvOS.

TV Library on an Apple TV views a library shared by a Mac, on the big screen.

Getting around

  • The sidebar has Search, the library views, Libraries and About. Genres are a row of buttons above the shows.
  • Hold Select on a tile, a season or an episode for its menu.
  • A show's cast is below its episodes: move down to reach it.

Libraries

The library you're viewing and the others you've used (press and hold one to rename or forget it), Connect to Another Library…, and the settings: Refresh Library, Artwork, Tile Size, Appearance, Language and Subtitle Position.

Playing

Episodes play full screen in the Apple TV's own player. Click left or right on the remote to skip 10 seconds; double-click Select to switch between filling the screen and the whole picture.

Top Shelf

With TV Library in the top row of the Home Screen, the Top Shelf shows the shows you watched last, each with its next episode and how far you got. Select opens the show; Play plays the episode.

Profiles

TV Library follows the Apple TV's profiles (Control Center › the profile). Each person signs in to their account once, and has their own libraries and Top Shelf. About › Sign In as Another Account… switches within one profile.

The Apple TV has no downloads, so when the Mac can't be reached it shows Couldn't Connect with Try Again.
