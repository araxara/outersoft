TV Library Viewer Help › Settings

Accounts settings

Choose TV Library Viewer › Settings, then click Accounts. Each person who watches gets an account, with their own watched episodes and resume points.

Add an account

  1. Click +, then enter a name and a password (at least 4 characters).
  2. The first account is the main one: its watching is the TV app's own, so watching in the TV app on this Mac counts for it. Every other account's watching is kept by TV Library Viewer and never changes the TV app.
Note: Once there's an account, everyone signs in with one. Devices that were connected are asked to sign in.

Manage accounts

Select an account, or Control-click it:

CommandWhat it does
Change Password…Sets a new password. Devices signed in with the old one ask for the new one.
Show Log…The account's statistics and its log of the last 30 days: connections, what was played and where it stopped, watched, removed, and refused passwords, each with the device and address.
Make MainMakes this the account whose watching is the TV app's.
Can remove episodesLets the account remove episodes from the library. The main account always can.
Delete…Deletes the account and its watching.

Statistics

For each account: whether it's connected now and from where, when it was last seen and from which device, total time connected, sessions, episodes watched in the last 30 days and wrong passwords.

Passwords aren't stored: only a check for each is kept, in this Mac's Keychain.
