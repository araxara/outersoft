Library views and genres

Mac, iPad, iPhone and Apple TV. On the Apple TV the genres are buttons above the shows.

The sidebar lists the views of your library. Under each view are its genres; choose one to see only those shows.

ViewShows
Recently AddedOne tile for each season added, newest first, under Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month, Last 3 Months, Last 6 Months, This Year and each earlier year. A season's tile opens the show at that season.
Recently WatchedEvery show you've played, the one you watched last first, under date headings. Stopping partway counts as watching.
TV ShowsEvery show, in the TV app's order: a leading "A", "An" or "The" is ignored, and titles starting with a number come last.
UnwatchedShows with no episode watched.
Partially WatchedShows you've started but not finished.
CompletedShows with every episode watched.
DownloadsEpisodes downloaded to this device (a shared library, on a Mac, iPad or iPhone). See Downloads and offline viewing.

The app opens on the view you used last. The search field (at the top of the sidebar on a Mac) finds shows by name in the view you're in.
