View menu

CommandWhat it does
Show Sidebar / Hide SidebarShows or hides the sidebar.
Bigger Tiles ⌘+
Smaller Tiles ⌘−
Actual Size Tiles ⌘0		 Makes the tiles bigger or smaller, from half to twice their size. Each view keeps its own size. On a show page this sizes the episodes' pictures.
ArtworkHorizontal, Vertical or Automatic: the shape of the shows' pictures. When a show has both shapes, the matching one is used.
AppearanceSystem, Light or Dark.
LanguageShows the app in one of 33 languages, or the Mac's own. The app offers to relaunch to change it. Show names and descriptions stay as they are in the library.
Enter Full ScreenFills the screen with the window.

The same controls are in the toolbar: the tile size slider, Artwork, Appearance and Refresh.
