TV Library Viewer Help › Settings

Sharing settings

Choose TV Library Viewer › Settings, then click Sharing.

SettingWhat it does
Share this Mac's TV libraryLets other devices browse the library, play its episodes, mark them watched or unwatched and remove them from the library. Changes are made in the TV app on this Mac.
StatusWhether it's sharing, under which name, and how many devices are connected.
CertificateThe fingerprint other devices show the first time they connect, to compare with this one.
Library passwordOne password for everyone. With accounts, it still works for older apps, as the main account; leave it empty to allow accounts only.
Port47100 unless you change it. Forward this port on your router to reach the library from another network. Leave it empty to let macOS choose.
Keep this Mac awake while sharingStops the Mac from sleeping while sharing is on.
