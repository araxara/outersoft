Mark watched, select and remove episodes

Episode rows

Each episode has its picture, with a check once watched (and the number of plays from 2), and a bar if it's in progress; then "EPISODE 3", its title, two lines of its description, and its length and date.

The episode menu

Control-click an episode, or click its ⋯:

  • Play
  • Get Info, Copy File Path, and Show in Finder on the Mac
  • Mark as Watched, or Add Watch and Mark as Unwatched
  • Download or Remove Download
  • Remove from Library…

Several at once

On a Mac, click, Shift-click, or press ⌘A. On an iPad or iPhone, tap Select. Or choose Select Season from a season's menu. The menu then acts on all of them: "Mark 3 Episodes as Watched", downloads, and Remove. Esc clears the selection.

Remove from the library

Remove from Library… (⌘⌫ on a Mac) asks first, then removes the entries from the TV app's library. The video files stay on disk. Undo (⌘Z) adds them back with their details and play counts; artwork added in the TV app comes back only if it's also in the file.

An account can remove episodes only if Can remove episodes is on for it.

Episodes only the Apple TV app can play

Episodes bought from Apple that are only in iCloud, or copy-protected, show "Apple TV App Only" with a lock. They're passed over by Play and downloads. On the Mac with the library, choosing one offers Play in Apple TV.
