Watch away from home

Your library stays on the Mac at home. Choose one way to reach it from elsewhere.

Through SSH

Best if you can already log in to that Mac, or a computer on its network, with SSH. Nothing else needs setting up. Use Remote (SSH) in Connect to Shared Library. Everything goes through the SSH connection.

Through a VPN

With a VPN such as Tailscale on both devices, use Address with the Mac's VPN address and port 47100.

Through a forwarded port

  1. On your router, forward port 47100 (or the port in Settings › Sharing) to the Mac with the library.
  2. Add accounts with strong passwords in Settings › Accounts.
  3. On the other device, use Address with your home's public address and the port.
Note: Connections are always encrypted, and devices check the library's certificate, wherever they connect from. Ten wrong passwords from one address within 5 minutes are refused without being checked.

With no connection at all

Download episodes before you go (Mac, iPad and iPhone). When the library can't be reached, the app opens it as last seen, shows Offline and plays your downloads. What you watch is sent to the Mac once it can be reached again.
