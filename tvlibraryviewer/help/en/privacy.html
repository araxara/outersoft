What TV Library Viewer reads, changes and keeps

Reads

The TV app's library and artwork, and the video files' own descriptions and pictures. Reading never changes them.

Changes

Only through the TV app: marking watched or unwatched, resume points, adding and removing library entries. Video files are never deleted.

Keeps

  • On the Mac with the library: a database of accounts, the watching of every account but the main one, 30 days of logs, full descriptions and cast (see Storage). Password checks and the sharing certificate are in its Keychain.
  • On viewing devices: the library as last seen (for offline use), downloads, and your account's name and password in the Keychain.
  • Sent elsewhere: only show names and the people in them, to TVmaze and TMDb, to look up casts and details. Your TMDb token goes only to TMDb. With a Google Cloud Translation key, titles and descriptions being translated into Greek, Czech, Hungarian, Romanian, Persian, Urdu or Bengali also go to Google; all other translation happens on the Mac.
