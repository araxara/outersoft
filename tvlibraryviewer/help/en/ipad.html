iPad and iPhone

iPadOS and iOS.

TV Library on an iPad or iPhone views a library shared by a Mac. It's laid out like the Mac app.

Getting around

  • The sidebar has the library views and your shared libraries. On an iPhone it's a screen of its own.
  • Touch and hold a tile or an episode (or right-click with a pointer) for its menu. Swipe an episode for the same commands.
  • The toolbar has the tile size, Artwork, Appearance, Refresh and About. On an iPhone they're in one View Options menu. An iPad does the same when there isn't room for them all.
  • Shake to undo on an iPhone.

With a keyboard

Hold ⌘, or swipe down from the top, for the menu bar: Library › Connect (⌘K), Refresh (⌘R), the views (⌘1–⌘8), and View › Bigger, Smaller and Actual Size Tiles (⌘+ ⌘− ⌘0).

Downloads

As on the Mac, with Smart Downloads. Downloads keep going in the background, and stay out of iCloud backups.

Apple Vision Pro

On Apple Vision Pro, episodes play in the system's player, which you can make bigger and take into an Environment from its controls.

Choose how episodes play with Theater › Watch in the toolbar or in the player's Viewing tab: In a Window, the standard player among your other windows; In the Theater, a movie theater around you; or Outdoors, the player full size, with the system's Environments from the button at its top left. The theater opens when an episode plays and closes with the player. Seat moves you anywhere from the front row to the back row, and Screen Height raises or lowers the screen.

An episode can also play in 3D: the Mac makes the 3D version from the episode as it streams. Playing an episode asks whether to Watch in 3D or Watch in 2D, and the next episodes play the same way. The 3D section of the player's Viewing tab sets the Depth, where the Screen sits in the picture (things coming out in front of it or going in behind it) and the Quality; a change applies at once, going on from where you are after a few seconds. The 3D version is at the episode's original size. Its subtitles are the system's own, at a fixed depth (Subtitle Position doesn't move them). It plays full size, in a window too, and closing it ends the episode. Making 3D keeps the Mac busy: two people can watch in 3D at once.

About

About has the library you're viewing, your account (statistics, devices, password), Accounts, and Keep a Log: a record of connections and playback you can copy to send when something goes wrong.
