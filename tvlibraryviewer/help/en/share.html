TV Library Viewer Help › Getting Started

Share your Mac's library

Do this once, on the Mac whose Apple TV app has your TV shows.

Give TV Library Viewer access

  1. Choose Apple menu › System Settings, then click Privacy & Security.
  2. Click Full Disk Access, then turn on TV Library Viewer. It needs this to read the TV app's library and artwork.
  3. The first time you mark an episode watched or remove one, macOS asks whether TV Library Viewer may control the TV app. Click Allow: that's how changes are made in the TV app.

Turn on sharing

  1. Choose TV Library Viewer › Settings, then click Sharing.
  2. Turn on Share this Mac's TV library.
  3. Note the Certificate fingerprint. The first time another device connects, it shows the same fingerprint for you to compare.

Devices on the same network now find the library by themselves. To protect it, add accounts in Settings › Accounts, or set a Library password.

Keep it reachable

  • Leave TV Library Viewer open. Sharing starts again by itself when it opens.
  • Keep this Mac awake while sharing stops the Mac from sleeping while sharing is on.
  • To watch from another network, see Watch away from home.
