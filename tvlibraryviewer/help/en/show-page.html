The show page and cast

Click a tile to open its show.

Details

The picture, the name, a line such as "2026 · Chinese Drama · 7 of 24 watched", and the description (click More for all of it).

  • Play plays the episode in progress, or else the next unwatched one. Its name says which: Continue Episode 7, Play Episode 15, or Play Again once you've seen them all. Space does the same.
  • ⋯ beside it has the tile menu's other commands. Control-clicking the header opens it too.

When the app is set to another language, episode titles and descriptions are translated into it on the Mac with the library, which keeps each translation so it's done only once. Titles made of the show's name, and text in a language the Mac can't translate into, stay as written.

Seasons

A show with more than one season lists each with how much is watched, and its own ⋯ menu: Play or Continue that season, Get Info, the watch commands, Select Season, Download Season and Remove.

Cast

Under the description (below the episodes on the Apple TV) is the show's cast: round photos with each actor's name and part, scrolling sideways, then the director, screenwriters and creator, with their job under their name. Choose someone to see their birth date, age, country, biography and other shows, with links to their pages on TVmaze and TMDb.

The cast comes from TVmaze and, with a TMDb token, from TMDb too: everyone either one lists, each person once. The Mac with the library looks a show up the first time its page is opened, and keeps the names, photos and page addresses. People's details are fetched when you open them, so they're up to date; the biography is in the app's language when TMDb has it, and in English otherwise. A show neither one has, or has no cast for yet, has no Cast row; it's looked up again a week later.
