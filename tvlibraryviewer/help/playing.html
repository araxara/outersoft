Play episodes

Where it picks up

An episode starts where you stopped. The place is saved every minute and when you pause or close it, so you can carry on on another device. Playing to the end, or skipping to the end and pressing Play, marks it watched and goes on to the show's next unwatched episode.

On a Mac each episode plays in its own window, whose title bar fades with the controls. The Mac, iPad and iPhone have Picture in Picture. The screen stays on while an episode plays.

If it stops

If the video stops arriving for a few seconds, the player loads it afresh at the same place, and tries a failed video again by itself.
