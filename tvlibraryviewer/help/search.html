Search and Spotlight

Spotlight: Mac, iPad and iPhone.

In the app

Type in the search field: at the top of the sidebar on a Mac, at the top of the view on an iPad or iPhone, and the Search tab on the Apple TV (typing or dictation). The view shows only the shows whose names match.

In Spotlight

On a Mac, iPad and iPhone, every show and episode of the library you're viewing is in Spotlight. Choosing one opens its show in the app, at the episode's season.
