Get Info

Choose Get Info from a tile's, a season's or an episode's menu.

The sections are Overview, Details, Artwork (every image, scrolling sideways), Description, Options, Sorting and File (size, codec, dimensions, bit rate and location). ‹ and › step through a season's episodes (⌘← ⌘→ on a Mac).

On a Mac the sections are listed on the left, as in the TV app. On an iPad, iPhone and Apple TV they're one list, and the iPad and iPhone have Copy All.
