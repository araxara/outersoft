Keeping the app up to date

TV Library Viewer updates itself. It checks once a day, and whenever you choose Check for Updates…, for a newer version; when there is one it shows what has changed and offers to download and install it, then reopens. Updates are verified before they're installed, so only genuine ones are accepted. The first time it checks it asks whether to do so automatically.

This is the Mac app, which you install yourself. The iPad and Apple TV apps update through the App Store, the way other apps on those devices do.
