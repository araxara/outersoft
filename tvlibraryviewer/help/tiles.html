Show tiles and the toolbar

Tiles

Each show's tile has its picture, its name and how many episodes you've watched ("7 of 24 Episodes Watched"), with a bar for an episode in progress and a badge when it's downloaded.

The tile menu

Control-click a tile (touch and hold on an iPad or iPhone, hold Select on the Apple TV):

  • Play Episode 5, Continue Episode 5 or Play Again, and Play First Episode
  • Get Info, Get Info for First Episode
  • Copy Folder Path, and Show in Finder on the Mac
  • Mark All as Watched, Add Watch to All, Mark All as Unwatched
  • Download Next 3 Unwatched, Download All Episodes, Remove Downloads
  • Remove from Library…

The toolbar

In the same order on every page: the tile size slider, Artwork (horizontal, vertical or automatic), Appearance (system, light or dark) and Refresh.
