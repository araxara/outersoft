iPad and iPhone

iPadOS and iOS.

TV Library on an iPad or iPhone views a library shared by a Mac. It's laid out like the Mac app.

Getting around

  • The sidebar has the library views and your shared libraries. On an iPhone it's a screen of its own.
  • Touch and hold a tile or an episode (or right-click with a pointer) for its menu. Swipe an episode for the same commands.
  • The toolbar has the tile size, Artwork, Appearance, Refresh and About. On an iPhone they're in one View Options menu.
  • Shake to undo on an iPhone.

With a keyboard

Hold ⌘, or swipe down from the top, for the menu bar: Library › Connect (⌘K), Refresh (⌘R), the views (⌘1–⌘8), and View › Bigger, Smaller and Actual Size Tiles (⌘+ ⌘− ⌘0).

Downloads

As on the Mac, with Smart Downloads. Downloads keep going in the background, and stay out of iCloud backups.

About

About has the library you're viewing, your account (statistics, devices, password), Sign In as Another Account…, and Keep a Log: a record of connections and playback you can copy to send when something goes wrong.
