Downloads and offline viewing

Mac, iPad and iPhone (and CarPlay plays them). The Apple TV has no downloads.

Download episodes from a shared library to watch them without a connection.

Download

  • An episode: click the download arrow on its row.
  • A season or a show: choose Download Season, Download Next 3 Unwatched or Download All Episodes from its ⋯ menu.

Downloads in the sidebar lists them by show, with their progress and size, and the space left. A download that's cut off carries on where it stopped. A downloaded episode always plays from this device's copy.

Settings

SettingWhat it does
Download on Wi-Fi onlyWaits for Wi-Fi (not a phone's hotspot). On by default.
Remove watched downloadsRemoves a download once you've watched it.
Smart DownloadsKeeps the next 1, 2, 3 or 5 unwatched episodes of each show you've played in the last 30 days downloaded, and removes each once it's watched.
Remove All…Removes every download. Only this device's copies are removed.

Offline

When the library's Mac can't be reached, the app opens the library as last seen with an Offline banner, plays only downloads, and keeps trying to reconnect (Try Again tries now). Resume points and watched episodes are kept and sent to the Mac, in order, once it answers.
