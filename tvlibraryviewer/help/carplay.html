CarPlay

iPhone with CarPlay.

With your iPhone connected to CarPlay, TV Library shows your library on the car's screen. It uses the library the iPhone is connected to.

Browse

Four tabs: Recently Watched, Recently Added, TV Shows and Downloads. Choose a show for its episodes and Play, Mark All as Watched; choose an episode for Play or Resume, Play from Beginning, Mark as Watched and Download.

Play

  • While driving you hear the episode, with Now Playing on the screen. Parked, a car that allows video shows the picture too. CarPlay decides which.
  • Up Next lists the show's following episodes, which play one after another.
  • Your place is saved as on the iPhone, so you can carry on at home. Leaving the car pauses it.

Episodes downloaded on the iPhone play with no connection.
