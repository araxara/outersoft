How TV Library Viewer works

One Mac keeps your TV shows. Everything else views them from that Mac.

The Mac with the library

The Mac whose Apple TV app holds your TV shows runs TV Library Viewer and shares its library. On that Mac, TV Library Viewer:

  • Reads the TV app's library and artwork directly, so it opens quickly and stays up to date when the TV app changes something.
  • Makes every change through the TV app: marking episodes watched, resume points and removing episodes. The TV app stays the one record of your library.
  • Shares the library with your other devices when Settings › Sharing is on, and keeps their accounts, watching, full descriptions and cast in its own database.
  • Has to be on and awake for the others to reach it. It stays awake by itself while sharing, unless you turn that off.
Note: Video files are never deleted. Removing an episode removes only its entry in the TV app's library; the file stays where it is.

The devices that view it

Every other device is a viewer. It has no library of its own; it shows the shared one, plays its episodes over the network and sends what you watch back to the Mac with the library.

All of them look and work alike: the same views, the same menus and the same words, laid out for each device.

Getting started

  1. On the Mac with the library, turn on sharing.
  2. If more than one person watches, add an account for each, so each keeps their own watching.
  3. On each other device, connect to the shared library.
