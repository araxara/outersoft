Menu bar, login and appearance

Small touches that keep the app out of your way.

The menu-bar item

Media Sharing GUI puts a small status item in the menu bar. Its tooltip shows the current state, and its menu has Show Window to bring the main window back when it's closed or the app is running quietly in the background.

Start at Login

Turn on Start at Login (in the app menu) to have Media Sharing GUI launch when you log in. The first time, macOS may ask you to approve it in System Settings › General › Login Items; the Log says if it does.

Appearance and language

  • The appearance button in the title bar switches between System, Light and Dark.
  • View › Language overrides the language just for this app; System means follow the Mac's own language setting. Changing it restarts the app to apply.
