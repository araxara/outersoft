Traffic and the log

Two boxes show how the connection is doing: how much it's moving, and what it's done.

SSH Session Traffic

  • In and Out show bytes flowing through the tunnel process, with a Peak figure for the busiest moment.
  • The numbers are sampled with the system tool nettop, not a raw packet capture, so they're a close guide rather than an exact meter.
  • How often it re-samples is set by Traffic poll interval in Configuration. Each sample takes a few seconds to run; a shorter interval than that just runs samples back to back.

Log

The Log box records each step — opening the tunnel, finding libraries, publishing them, reconnecting, and any errors. It's also written to a file on disk; the box shows Saved to: its path. Click that path to reveal the log in Finder, handy when you want to send it along while sorting out a problem.
